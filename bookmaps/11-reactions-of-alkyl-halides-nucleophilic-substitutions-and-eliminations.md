## Exercises — provenance

The four-tier exercise structure (warm-up / application / synthesis / challenge) is a workshop convention layered over OpenStax's "Problems" sections at the end of each module and the chapter's "Additional Problems."

- **11.1–11.3 (warm-up)** — direct nucleophilicity and steric ordering. Stems analogous to OpenStax problems in m00122, m00123, m00124.
- **11.4 (application)** — the optical-activity diagnostic for SN1 vs. SN2. Construction in spirit of OpenStax m00125.
- **11.5 (application)** — four-substrate prediction. Mirrors OpenStax m00133 "Predicting the Product and Mechanism of Reactions" example.
- **11.6 (application)** — *cis*- and *trans*-1-bromo-2-methylcyclohexane. Construction in spirit of OpenStax m00130 menthyl/neomenthyl analysis, but on a more accessible substrate.
- **11.7–11.9 (synthesis)** — design problems. New constructions extending the chapter's worked examples.
- **11.10 (challenge)** — partial racemization with residual inversion bias. Tests understanding of ion pairs from m00125.
- **11.11 (challenge)** — cyclopentane vs. cyclohexane geometry for E2. Constructed; the cyclopentane case is not in OpenStax m00130.
- **11.12 (challenge)** — E1cB vs. E1 under acid catalysis. Constructed extension of m00131 + m00132.

All exercise prompts can be answered using only material in the chapter; no additional readings required.

## Deferred content (not in chapter, retained here for future use)

**Green Chemistry sidebar (m00133, ~750 words).**

The OpenStax chapter ends with a sidebar on green chemistry — the 12 principles, the ibuprofen process improvement (7 million pounds/year, 99% less waste, three steps, recovered HF solvent). Excluded from the chapter rewrite because:

1. It's tangential to the mechanism core of the chapter.
2. The 12-principle list is a standalone topic that fits more naturally as an essay or as an end-of-book chapter on industrial synthesis.
3. The chapter is already 7,485 words; including the sidebar would push it to ~8,200, beyond the 5,000–8,000 target.

**Recommended reuse:** as a `/essay` or `/nart` topic, or as material for a final-chapter or appendix on synthesis design and environmental impact. The ibuprofen example is reusable as a hook for sustainability essays and for chapter on synthetic design.

**Tosylate stereochemistry detail (m00122, three-step 1-phenyl-2-propanol cycle).**

The OpenStax module includes a detailed three-step cycle interconverting the enantiomers of 1-phenyl-2-propanol via a tosylate intermediate, used to identify which step inverts the configuration. The chapter compresses this to one sentence ("similar logic on a tosylate substrate gives the same conclusion"). The full diagrammatic version of the cycle is reusable as a homework problem or as a worked example in a stereochemistry chapter.

**Detailed kinetics derivation (m00123).**

The OpenStax source presents the rate equation derivation in compact form. The chapter rewrite uses it as-is. A more pedagogically expanded version (showing the rate-law derivation step by step from molecular collision theory) is recommended for the Chapter 6 (overview of organic reactions) rewrite, where kinetics is the primary subject.
